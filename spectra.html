Fe K absorption search in the brightest local AGN of 4XMM-DR14: EPIC-pn spectra of all the observations

483 observations of 165 AGN (the sample observation of each source and 318 further observations). One vector PDF per observation (text searchable); all of them in one file: all_spectra.pdf. Click a column header to sort; type in the box to filter.

Each panel: top, EPIC-pn data (PPS spectrum, optimal binning), adopted model (solid) and its additive components (dashed); middle, data / continuum (absorption lines removed); bottom, data / full model. Vertical lines: red solid, UFO absorption lines (7.20-12 keV rest, i.e. outflow velocity above 10,000 km/s as Fe XXVI Ly-alpha, as in Tombesi et al. 2010) detected at >= 95 per cent Monte Carlo confidence; orange dashed, UFO candidates below 95 per cent (Delta C >= 8 but not significant); green dashed, near-systemic Fe XXV / Fe XXVI absorption (6.5-7.20 keV rest, or reclassified by the Fe K edge rule [k] or as the He-beta / Ly-beta line of a near-systemic He-alpha / Ly-alpha line [b], labelled He/Ly alpha and beta); violet, Fe XXV He-alpha + Fe XXVI Ly-alpha pair of one absorber at a common velocity (labelled XXV / XXVI; counted as one UFO, listed once in the title with the confidence of its stronger line [p]; a pair below the near-systemic velocity, 0.033c = 10,000 km/s, would be green dashed); grey dash-dot, lines at the band edge, never counted; dotted, 6.4, 6.7 and 6.97 keV rest. The title line gives the detected UFO lines with their Monte Carlo confidence (cont.-dep.: continuum dependent [c]). Energies are rest frame (keV). Flags in the table: c, continuum dependent; k, Fe K edge rule; b, K-beta rule; v, K-beta of a near-systemic line at 0.026c, slightly above the 0.025c limit of the K-beta rule (flagged also b); p, Fe XXV Heα + Fe XXVI Lyα pair at a common velocity.
