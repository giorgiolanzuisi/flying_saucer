Column descriptions
Source
ObsID
linked to the spectrum
Use
sample / other epoch
Baseline
absorbed PL: absorbed power law with MYTorus reflection; MYTorus: decoupled MYTorus model
Components
optional components: R free reflection; Sc scattered power law; SPL soft power law; PC neutral partial covering; WA, 2WA one or two warm (ionised) absorbers; bFe blurred Fe Kα; Fe25, Fe26, Ni Fe XXV Heα, Fe XXVI Lyα and Ni Kα emission lines; EmX emission line of free energy; in brackets: pn energy-scale shift or Gaussian Fe Kα and Kβ lines in place of the MYTorus line table (see the paper, appendix on the fit quality)
Tier
data: data-driven selection; literature: literature-informed (published absorbers / reflection as starting values); literature-based: continuum built from the published broadband model
Emin (keV)
lower limit of the fitted band (rest frame); the upper limit is 12 keV
Γ
photon index (in brackets: fixed)
log NH
line-of-sight column density (cm-2; <20: below 1020)
AS
reflection normalisation (MYTorus model, or absorbed power law with free reflection)
fs
scattered fraction
Γsoft
photon index of the soft power law
Absorbers
further absorbers: PC log NH/covering fraction; WA log NH/log ξ/covering fraction; NH,S: column density of the reflector where fitted separately from the line of sight
C
C-statistic of the continuum fit (without absorption lines)
dof
degrees of freedom
Literature anchors
published broadband fits used to anchor the continuum (references below)
