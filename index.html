How common are ultra-fast outflows, and how often do they switch on?

SAUCER searches the XMM-Newton archive for the Fe K absorption lines of ultra-fast outflows (UFOs) in a homogeneous, count-limited sample of 163 local AGN, calibrates every line with simulations and uses 318 repeated observations to separate where the winds are (covering factor) from when they are there (duty cycle).
